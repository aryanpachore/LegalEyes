Can you help me understand what an NDA is and what it should include?

Absolutely! An NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement) is a legal contract that establishes a confidential relationship between parties.

Key components of an NDA:

  • • Definition of confidential information
  • • Obligations of receiving party
  • • Time period of confidentiality
  • • Exclusions from confidential information
  • • Consequences of breach
